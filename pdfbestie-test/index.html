🔒 Privacy First Processing

**Everything processed locally on your device.**

PDF Bestie is a **100% client-side** application. This means all your document merging, splitting, and editing operations happen right in your web browser using JavaScript.

**No Server Uploads, Logs, or Tracking of Documents.**

Your sensitive documents are never transmitted to our servers. We never see, store, or log the contents of your PDFs. Once you close the tab, the temporary data is gone.
